# Version 0.7.0 Stage 4

Implemented:

- Repair is a city order.
- Demolition is a city order and does not refund resources.
- Active city orders can be cancelled.
- Cancelling an order refunds resources based on remaining progress.
- Minor orders process during settlement cycles.
- Ability rewards can grant linked reward Items.
- Ability active effects are tracked by settlement cycles.
- Active effects are reported to chat each cycle.
- Players can donate gp from their Actor to settlement treasury.
- Failed defense can damage or destroy a random building.
- Dark styling for build picker and ability/resident forms.

Notes:

- Full native dnd5e ActiveEffect application can be expanded later.
